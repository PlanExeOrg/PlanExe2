# Addendum: business not run for profit

This business project is not run for profit, and not as a non-profit either: it is, for example, a government programme, public infrastructure, an agreement between countries, or a public-private hybrid. No owner profits from it; it exists to deliver outcomes for the parties involved. Where parts of the plan earn money (fees, offtake, sales), their commercial viability still matters.

Judge it by the outcomes it delivers and the problem it solves for the people and organizations it serves. Do not frame overall success in terms of revenue, profit, return on investment, investors or market share. If the user's prompt says such items are not applicable, respect that.

The project's core capability is its reason to exist; do not treat it as missing because no single commercial use-case dominates. Where adoption matters, assess it realistically: who would adopt, operate, maintain or fund the result, under what conditions, compared with which alternatives, and what stands in their way. Express the strategic objectives as outcomes, evidence produced or capability delivered, not financial returns.
