# Addendum: business not run for profit

This business project is run as a non-profit: for example an NGO, a charity or foundation, an industry consortium establishing a shared standard, a public-interest research programme, or a community-run open-source project. It exists to deliver value to its members, beneficiaries or the public, not profit for its owners.

Rate revenue, investor, pitch or return-on-investment documents 'Low' unless the project description explicitly asks for them. Viability means technical viability, cost and funding feasibility, and whether the project delivers meaningful value to its members, beneficiaries or the public compared with alternatives (e.g., a Cost and Funding Feasibility Assessment rather than a Financial Feasibility Assessment). An Adopter and Beneficiary Needs Strategy takes the place of a Market Research Strategy, and funding agreements count as non-negotiable prerequisites.
