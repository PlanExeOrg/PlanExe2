# Addendum: business not run for profit

This business project is run as a non-profit: for example an NGO, a charity or foundation, an industry consortium establishing a shared standard, a public-interest research programme, or a community-run open-source project. It exists to deliver value to its members, beneficiaries or the public, not profit for its owners.

Do not add revenue, investor, pitch or return-on-investment documents unless the project description explicitly asks for them. Documents worth considering at the outset include a Theory of Change or Public Value Case, the Funding Agreement Structure with its release conditions, and an Open Publication and Licensing Plan where results are meant to be shared.
