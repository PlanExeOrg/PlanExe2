# Addendum: business not run for profit

This business project is run as a non-profit: for example an NGO, a charity or foundation, an industry consortium establishing a shared standard, a public-interest research programme, or a community-run open-source project. It exists to deliver value to its members, beneficiaries or the public, not profit for its owners.

Money flow is not a purpose signal: grants, public budgets, donations and large programme budgets do not make the project commercial; classify it by what it actually does. For a programme serving a population, community or beneficiary group, prefer the specialist practice or non-profit specialty that delivers its outcome over the broad policy area it sits in (the policy area, if relevant, is a secondary entry); for a technical programme, the engineering or scientific discipline that delivers the core capability usually owns the outcome.
